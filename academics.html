Academics

Curriculum designed for strong basics and future exams

01

Nursery to Class V

LEAD curriculum as per NEP 2020 with detailed lesson plans, well-divided syllabus, audio-video TLMs, live examples, and TV-enabled classroom learning.

02

Classes VI to X

SCERT board syllabus with IIT and NEET foundation support from LEAD curriculum to prepare students for higher-level competitive exams.

03

Digital Learning

Nursery to Class V students use the LEAD Student App for revision at home, while Classes VI to IX use the SpeedLabs app for IIT foundation learning.

ROA Curriculum

Classes III to V receive ROA curriculum support to build early aptitude and confidence for IIT-oriented learning.

Reasoning for Competitive Exams

From this year, reasoning is introduced as an important subject. For Classes I to V, it supports preparation for Navodaya, Sainik School, TSRJC, and Gurukul exams. For Classes VI to IX, it helps students prepare for government and other competitive exams where reasoning carries high marks.
