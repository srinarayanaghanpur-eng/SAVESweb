About

Sri Adarshavani Vidyanikethan High School

Sri Adarshavani Vidyanikethan High School provides a safe, disciplined, and inspiring environment for students from primary to senior classes. Established in 2010, our teachers focus on conceptual learning, respectful behavior, and individual attention so each child can discover their strengths. Hostel facilities are also available for students.
